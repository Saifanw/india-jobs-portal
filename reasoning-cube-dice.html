Part 1 — Concepts, rules & microscopic explanation

Concept 1: Cube & Dice — Rule 1

Standard die opposite faces sum to 7 only when the question specifies a standard die; never assume for arbitrary dice.

Concept 2: Cube & Dice — Rule 2

Two views with one common face in the same position can help determine opposite faces; rotate views mentally and compare neighbors.

Concept 3: Cube & Dice — Rule 3

A face cannot be adjacent to its opposite. Each cube face has four adjacent faces and one opposite face.

Concept 4: Cube & Dice — Rule 4

Painted cube cut into n×n×n smaller cubes: 3-face painted corners=8; 2-face painted edge cubes=12(n−2); 1-face painted face-centers=6(n−2)²; unpainted=(n−2)³.

Concept 5: Cube & Dice — Rule 5

For non-cubic cuts or different dimensions, count faces/edges/corners by dimensions rather than applying n-cube formulas blindly.

Exam-smart habit: Kisi pattern ko answer par force mat karein. Rule ko saare given terms par check karein; agar multiple rules fit hote hain, options aur question wording se exact intended relation verify karein.

Part 2 — Solved examples

In examples ko answer dekhe bina solve karein. Phir explanation padhkar apni reasoning compare karein.

Example 1: On a standard die, opposite of 2?

Options: A) 3 | B) 4 | C) 5 | D) 6

Answer: C) 5

Step-by-step: Standard die opposite pairs sum to 7; 2 opposite 5.

Example 2: A cube is painted all over and cut 3×3×3. How many small cubes have 3 painted faces?

Options: A) 4 | B) 6 | C) 8 | D) 12

Answer: C) 8

Step-by-step: Eight corner cubes each have three painted faces.

Example 3: For n=4 painted cube, cubes with no paint?

Options: A) 4 | B) 8 | C) 16 | D) 27

Answer: B) 8

Step-by-step: Interior count=(4−2)³=8.
