Part 1 — Concepts, rules & microscopic explanation

Concept 1: Clock — Rule 1

Minute hand speed = 6° per minute; hour hand = 0.5° per minute. Relative speed = 5.5° per minute.

Concept 2: Clock — Rule 2

At h:m, angle difference = |30h − 5.5m| degrees; smaller angle = min(angle, 360°−angle). Use h modulo 12.

Concept 3: Clock — Rule 3

Hands coincide when relative angle closes; approximately every 65 5/11 minutes.

Concept 4: Clock — Rule 4

Right angle (90°), straight angle (180°) and opposite directions require solving relative-angle equations, not visual guessing.

Concept 5: Clock — Rule 5

Clock gaining/losing questions: rate error per hour/day ko actual elapsed time ke saath scale karo.

Exam-smart habit: Kisi pattern ko answer par force mat karein. Rule ko saare given terms par check karein; agar multiple rules fit hote hain, options aur question wording se exact intended relation verify karein.

Part 2 — Solved examples

In examples ko answer dekhe bina solve karein. Phir explanation padhkar apni reasoning compare karein.

Example 1: Angle at 3:00?

Options: A) 0° | B) 45° | C) 90° | D) 180°

Answer: C) 90°

Step-by-step: Hour at 90°, minute at 0°; difference 90°.

Example 2: Minute hand moves in 10 minutes by?

Options: A) 30° | B) 60° | C) 90° | D) 120°

Answer: B) 60°

Step-by-step: 6° per minute ×10=60°.

Example 3: At 6:00 smaller angle?

Options: A) 0° | B) 90° | C) 180° | D) 270°

Answer: C) 180°

Step-by-step: Hands opposite; 180°.
