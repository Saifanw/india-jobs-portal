Part 1 — Concepts, rules & microscopic explanation

Concept 1: Paper Folding & Cutting — Rule 1

Fold sequence ko reverse order mein unfold karo: last fold first open, then previous folds.

Concept 2: Paper Folding & Cutting — Rule 2

Har fold symmetry axis banata hai. Hole/cut unfold hone par axis ke across mirror copies create karta hai.

Concept 3: Paper Folding & Cutting — Rule 3

Paper orientation aur fold direction (vertical, horizontal, diagonal) carefully track karo.

Concept 4: Paper Folding & Cutting — Rule 4

Cut edge par ho to unfold copies boundary se clipped ho sakti hain; interior hole generally reflected copies banata hai.

Concept 5: Paper Folding & Cutting — Rule 5

Options mein symmetry, number of holes, relative spacing and mirror position compare karo.

Part 2 — Solved examples

In examples ko answer dekhe bina solve karein. Phir explanation padhkar apni reasoning compare karein.

Example 1: Paper folded once in half; one hole punched away from fold. On opening, holes?

Options: A) 1 | B) 2 | C) 3 | D) 4

Answer: B) 2

Step-by-step: Single fold creates a mirrored copy: 2 holes.

Example 2: Two perpendicular folds; one interior punch. How many copies on full opening?

Options: A) 2 | B) 3 | C) 4 | D) 8

Answer: C) 4

Step-by-step: Two folds create 2×2=4 symmetric copies, assuming punch not on fold.

Example 3: A fold is opened in reverse order. First unfold which fold?

Options: A) First made | B) Last made | C) Middle | D) Any

Answer: B) Last made

Step-by-step: Undo the most recent fold first.
